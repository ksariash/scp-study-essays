Prototype idea

Sentence jobs, not self-grading

The app tells you what a strong sentence must do, but you must retrieve the authorities and key concepts yourself.

  1. Read the sentence job.
  2. Fill the key terms from memory.
  3. Check the sentence.
  4. Correct mistakes or request a cue.
  5. Watch the full essay assemble as you progress.

The five questions and source answers come from the supplied Essay Study Guide. Sentence wording is lightly reshaped for this experiment without changing the source content.
